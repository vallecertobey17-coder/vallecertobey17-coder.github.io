OUR STORY & WORKSHOP BACKGROUND

Abay-Abay Carburetor & Engine Repair was established to meet a specific, recurring need in local roads: reliable, accurate mechanical repair for hardworking utility vehicles without the surrounding noise and markups from the larger chain shops.

Our work is centered around carburetor calibration, engine diagnostics, and preventive maintenance for multicabs, mini cabs, and other compact utility vehicles.

Rather than adopting a generalized or speculative service model, the workshop committed its bay space and bench tooling to mastering the exact mechanics behind light-duty combustion engines. From stripping down clogged multi-barrel carburetors to resolving stubborn vacuum leaks and low-cylinder pressure, the shop operates on straightforward diagnostic technique: check the problem directly, explain the remedy clearly, and only perform the work that the vehicle genuinely requires.
